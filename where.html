Fast-growing areas are under pressure

Drury, Takanini, Papakura, and Manukau sit between Auckland's urban centre and the rural Franklin district, making them key locations for future development.

Important affected ecosystems

Environmental impacts can already be seen around important natural areas such as Ōtara Creek, Puhinui Reserve, and the Manukau Harbour catchment. These areas connect land, water, and habitat across South Auckland.

Drury has been identified as a major future growth area and could eventually house around 60,000 new residents. This growth supports housing needs, but it also increases pressure on nearby ecosystems.
